## Goal
Determine whether extraction orientation alone changes two-qubit gate count while preserving circuit equivalence and without increasing T-count. Distinguish the extraction effect from ZX-graph simplification effects.

## Experiment
**Planned, not yet executed:** Generate 30 independent 6-qubit `CNOT_HAD_PHASE_circuit` instances (generator depth 80, `p_had=0.2`, `p_t=0.2`, seeds 0–29). For each circuit, run `full_reduce` once to obtain a *shared* reduced ZX-diagram `G`, then extract (a) forward from `G.copy()` and (b) reverse from `G.adjoint()`, restoring the output using `.adjoint()`. Hold `optimize_cnots=2`, `optimize_czs=True`, `up_to_perm=False` and software versions fixed. Verify both candidates.

**Work reviewed today:** Earlier pilot `pilot-f2-r2-seed-20261115` with PyZX 0.9.0; 6 qubits; original circuit seed 20261115.

## Discussion
The pilot runs `full_reduce` separately on the original circuit and the adjoint circuit before extraction. Therefore, the observed 2Q difference does **not** isolate extraction orientation; separate simplification trajectories may contribute. A controlled experiment using a common reduced ZX-diagram is required. The approximately 0.4% median runtime difference is too small to interpret from three short timings.
