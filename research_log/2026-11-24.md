## Discussion

The screening found the desired contrasting examples. However, “phase circuits benefit from ZX” is too broad: the fixed-length 4-qubit phase inputs improved strongly, while all 8-qubit phase inputs increased 2Q. Width, operations per qubit, parity reuse, and extraction overhead are plausible confounds; this screening does not identify a causal factor. All inputs are exploratory and were generated by narrowly specified synthetic recipes. Three seeds do not establish general success probabilities.

The paired baseline matters. The Toffoli results reduce T at the cost of additional 2Q and depth; they cannot be labeled an unconditional improvement. The Pauli results show that fewer 2Q gates can still require greater logical depth. Some full-pipeline wins appear only after cleanup: `phase_6q_s0` has 39 pre-cleanup 2Q versus Gate-only's 38, then 34 after cleanup; `clifford_t_6q_s0` has 38 versus 37, then 33. These observations motivate process tracing rather than attributing every gain to ZX rewriting alone.

No hardware routing, noise, physical execution time, approximate Clifford+T synthesis of QFT, exact symbolic equivalence proof, or independent external rerun was performed. Numerical verification establishes agreement to the recorded tolerance. Timeout/resource failure paths are implemented but were not triggered by this successful run. The result concerns this PyZX version, pipeline, basis, and circuit set, not a universal limit of ZX-calculus.
