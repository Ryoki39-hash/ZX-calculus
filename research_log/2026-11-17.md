## Discussion

The controlled eight-input pilot eliminates the separate-simplification confound within each input, but gives no strict R2 2Q improvement at extraction level 2. It is too small and synthetic to establish that reverse extraction is generally inferior. The 6-qubit Toffoli ladder illustrates a trade-off: reverse lowers depth by eight while increasing 2Q by five, with unchanged T-count. Gate-basis normalization, input identity, PyZX version, verification tolerance, and timing scope must remain fixed before broader comparisons.

The existing CSV stores timing medians, not raw distributions or measured full portfolio runtime. Consequently, precise runtime uncertainty and end-to-end selection overhead cannot yet be inferred. Site demo data are not experiment evidence.

### Verification and provenance limitations identified in the audit

The script numerically compares the **initial** F2 and R2 output matrices against the input using a unit-magnitude global phase, fixed wire order, `atol=1e-9`, and `rtol=0`. During timing repetitions, it checks only that T-count, 2Q count, and depth match the initial candidates. Matching these three metrics does not itself establish that every repeated output implements the same unitary. The recorded 16 successful checks must not be presented as independent matrix verification of all timed outputs. The numerical residuals, output hashes, and raw timing samples are not retained in the CSV.

The script also uses assertions and writes the CSV only after completing the input loop. A future assertion failure could interrupt a sweep without saving partial results. A robust sweep needs explicit per-input failure records and incremental persistence, rather than reporting only completed successful runs. This is a code-level risk, not evidence that a hidden failure occurred in the recorded pilot.

The four Toffoli ladders are structurally related, and the other family contains only four random seeds. The eight distinct circuits should not be treated as an independent random sample of a broad circuit population when estimating uncertainty. Timing repetitions are a separate level of replication. No F3/R3 measurements, held-out results, or new daily performance trend are established by this repository snapshot.

The older single-input result remains evidence carried forward from the prior journal; its external library artifacts were not independently re-read in this audit. Differences between that protocol and the committed pilot must not be interpreted as a chronological regression. The data here support a measured absence of strict R2 gains on eight specific inputs, not a proof that reverse extraction cannot help.
